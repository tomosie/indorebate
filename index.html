Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us on Customer Service or by email.

What is a rebate?
A rebate is a share of the broker's commission passed on to traders through a partner such as Indorebate. It is paid automatically, whether your trades win or lose, as long as the broker's conditions are met. Read more on What is Rebate.
Is there a fee, and do my trading conditions change?
There is no registration fee and no extra commission. Your trading conditions are the same as registering directly with the broker, and you still deposit and withdraw directly with the broker.
I already have a broker account. Do I still get rebates?
An existing account that wasn't registered through an Indorebate link does not automatically earn rebates, and validating it alone is not enough. You need to switch the account's partner to Indorebate or open a new account through our link. The steps differ by broker, so start by requesting a partner switch.
What is validation and how does it work?
Validation confirms that your trading account is under the Indorebate network. Fill in the validation form with your broker, account number, and email. We check the account in the broker's portal and email you the result. Check your Spam or Promotions folder too. If the account isn't under Indorebate yet, the result email explains how to switch partners.
When and how are rebates paid?
Rebates go into your trading account automatically, with no manual request, on a T+1 basis (the day after you trade), as long as each broker's rebate conditions are met. Tickmill is the exception: it pays once a week, every Monday. Some brokers also set a minimum payout, for example HFM pays rebates once the amount reaches $1.
How much rebate will I get?
You receive 80% of the partner commission. The amount depends on the broker, account type, pair, and your trading volume. Use the rebate calculator for an estimate.
When does a trade not earn a rebate?
In general, trading with a bonus or margin bonus earns no rebate. Each broker also has its own limits, such as minimum order duration and minimum accumulated lots on cent accounts. Open the broker list and click Terms & conditions on your broker.
Does Indorebate hold my funds?
No. Indorebate is not part of any broker and does not hold client funds. Your money stays with the broker. Remember that forex trading carries a risk of losing funds, so use your money wisely.
How do I get the free tools?
The free tools are available to members with an active, validated account at a partner broker. Once your account is approved, the admin sends an activation password that you enter into the EA's parameters along with your trading account number. Details are on the Free Tools page.
How can I contact Indorebate?
Through our Customer Service or by email at support@indorebate.com.
