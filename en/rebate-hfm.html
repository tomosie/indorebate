What Is HFM Rebate?

HFM rebate (cashback) is a refund of part of the spread or commission you pay the broker every time an order is closed — whether the order ends in profit or loss. This money comes from the share of commission that partners usually receive, and Indorebate passes it back to traders as a rebate.

"To earn a rebate from Indorebate, there is no spread markup or additional commission, and trading conditions at the broker stay the same as a regular account."

HFM Rebate Terms & Conditions

  • Orders closed in under 2 minutes do not earn rebates.
  • Trades with a lot size that is too small on Cent accounts earn no rebate because the rebate value falls below $0.01.
  • For cent accounts, a minimum accumulated volume of 0.2 lots is required to earn rebates.
  • Rebates are paid once the rebate amount reaches $1.
  • Rebates only apply to trading accounts registered under the Indorebate network.
  • Opening a live account through Indorebate does not change the broker's trading conditions — there is no spread/commission markup or hidden fees.
