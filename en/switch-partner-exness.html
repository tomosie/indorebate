A guide to moving your account to the Indorebate partner so you can enjoy the cashback rebate program.

Method 1 - Switch Partner via Exness Live Chat

1

Contact Exness Live Chat

Type the message:

CHANGE PARTNERS
2

Open the Link from Exness

Click the link provided, then log in to your Exness account.

Reason to select: Rebates
3

Enter the Partner Link

https://one.toexness.com/intl/id/a/indorbt80
4

Fill in "Where did you find your new partner?"

Any answer is fine — pick whichever best matches how you found us, for example: Social Media, Internet, TikTok, Google, YouTube, or Friend/Referral. The "Leave a comment" field can be left empty. Then click Submit.

5

Once the Status Is ACCEPTED

Check your email regularly. Exness will send an approval email to the address registered on your account. Once the status changes to ACCEPTED, create a new Real Trading account, then validate that new account number on the Indorebate website.

Validate Account Now →
Important: Your old trading account does not move over. Only a Real Trading account created after the status is Accepted joins the Indorebate partner and earns rebates. After the new account is created, validate that new account number, not the old one.
