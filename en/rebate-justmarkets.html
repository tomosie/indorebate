What Is JustMarkets Rebate?

JustMarkets rebate (cashback) is a refund of part of the spread or commission you pay the broker every time an order is closed — whether the order ends in profit or loss. This money comes from the share of commission that partners usually receive, and Indorebate passes it back to traders as a rebate.

"To earn a rebate from Indorebate, there is no spread markup or additional commission, and trading conditions at the broker stay the same as a regular account."

JustMarkets Rebate Terms & Conditions

  • Trading with margin bonus does not earn rebates.
  • Orders closed under 59 points or 5.9 pips (the difference between open and close price) do not earn rebates.
  • Trades with a lot size that is too small on cent accounts earn no rebate because the rebate falls below $0.01.
  • Metal pairs have no 59-point MTP (Minimum Trade Points), but a 2-minute MTM (Minimum Trade Minutes) applies.
  • Rebates only apply to trading accounts registered under the Indorebate network.
  • Opening a live account through Indorebate does not change the broker's trading conditions — there is no spread/commission markup or hidden fees.
