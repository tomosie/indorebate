HOW TO OPEN AN ACCOUNT OF ANOTHER TYPE

Already have a TMGM account and want to open an additional account of a different type (for example, you have a Standard account and want to add a Cent account)? Follow TMGM's official guide below.

⚠ Important Note

This is not a process of changing the type of an existing account, but rather opening a new additional account of a different type. You do not need to register yourself through the portal — just contact TMGM's official support team by email, and they will process the opening of the new account type for you.

The Steps

1
Email the TMGM Support Team

Send an email to support@tmgm.com and say that you want to open an additional account of a different type from your existing account. To make the process quick, include your account details — you can use the ready-made template below, just copy it and fill in the parts marked with square brackets [...].

To: support@tmgm.com
Subject: Request to Open a TMGM Account of Another Type
Hello TMGM Support Team, I already have a TMGM trading account, and I would like to open an additional account with a different account type. My Current Account Number: [ENTER YOUR ACCOUNT NUMBER] Full Name: [ENTER YOUR FULL NAME] Registered Email: [ENTER YOUR ACCOUNT EMAIL] Desired New Account Type: [ENTER ACCOUNT TYPE, e.g.: EDGE / Classic / STD — Cent / USD] I would appreciate your help with opening this account. Thank you. Best regards, [Your Name]
✉ Open in Email App ✓ Template copied!
2
Wait for TMGM Support to Contact You

After the email is sent, the TMGM support team will contact you directly (not through Indorebate) and guide you through opening the new account type until it is done.

ℹ️ Opening this new account type is handled purely by the TMGM team directly. If you run into problems while waiting for TMGM's response, you can also let the Indorebate admin know via Telegram (t.me/tomosie) so we can help follow up.
