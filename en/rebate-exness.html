What Is Exness Rebate?

Exness rebate (cashback) is a refund of part of the spread or commission you pay the broker every time an order is closed — whether the order ends in profit or loss. This money comes from the share of commission that partners usually receive, and Indorebate passes it back to traders as a rebate.

"To earn a rebate from Indorebate, there is no spread markup or additional commission, and trading conditions at the broker stay the same as a regular account."
