About Indorebate

A multi-broker rebate partner helping forex traders get part of their trading costs back automatically.

Who we are

Indorebate has operated since 2020 from Singapore as an Introducing Broker (IB) and an official partner of our partner brokers. We connect traders with selected brokers and share 80% of the partner commission with traders as rebates paid automatically.

What we do

  • Vet brokers

    We only work with brokers that run an official auto-rebate system.

  • Share rebates automatically

    Rebates go into your trading account with no manual request, under the same trading conditions as registering directly with the broker.

  • Support our members

    We help with account validation and partner switching, and answer questions on Telegram and by email.

Our position

Indorebate is not a broker and does not hold client funds. Deposits, withdrawals, and trading are done directly with the broker. We are only a partner offering convenience and additional facilities to clients.

Our commitments

  • Transparent. The 80% rebate share and each broker's terms are openly available on this site.
  • No extra cost. No registration fee and no extra commission for traders.
  • Easy to reach. Our team can be contacted on Telegram and by email.
