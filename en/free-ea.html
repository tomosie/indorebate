Important: This EA Is a Tool, Not a Profit Guarantee

Before using it, make sure you fully understand how it works and its risks. This EA is built to assist the trading process, not to replace your strategy and your decisions as a trader.

⚠️ What You Must Understand

  • The EA does not analyze the market and does not decide when to enter. The initial BUY/SELL decision is entirely manual via the panel buttons, based on your own analysis.
  • After the first entry, the EA runs with two automatic modes: Pyramid — adds new positions when price moves in the profit direction by the Pyramid Distance, then locks in basket profit with a trailing stop at the Pyramid SL distance; and Averaging/Martingale — adds positions with multiplied lots (multiplier) when price moves against the initial position by the Grid Distance, to chase break-even (BEP).
  • Averaging mode is high risk. If the adverse trend continues without a reversal and is not managed with discipline (lot size, grid distance, number of orders relative to your capital), your account may hit a Margin Call (MC).
  • This EA does not run automatically 24 hours a day. You still need to monitor your positions, market conditions, and economic news — including while the automatic re-entry feature is active.
  • Trading results depend entirely on each user's strategy, risk management, and discipline — not on the EA itself.
These tools are intended for traders who already understand the concepts of pyramiding, martingale/averaging, and their risks. Indorebate is not responsible for trading losses arising from the use of these tools. Forex trading carries a risk of losing capital.
Features

What This EA Does

The EA helps automate technical matters after you decide to enter — it does not make trading decisions for you.

FeaturesDescription
Manual Entry via Order PanelBUY NOW / SELL NOW buttons right on the chart — you decide the direction and timing of the first entry.
Auto PyramidWhen price moves in the profit direction by the Pyramid Distance, the EA adds a new position following the trend, then immediately places a basket stop loss at the Pyramid SL distance behind price — and trails it every time a new pyramid order is formed.
Auto Averaging / MartingaleWhen price moves against the position by the Grid Distance, the EA adds a position with multiplied lots according to the Multiplier to chase break-even (BEP).
Auto Basket TPOnce averaging mode is active, the EA calculates the BEP of the whole basket and automatically places a Take Profit at the Basket TP distance from that BEP.
Trailing Stop PyramidWhile positions are still in pyramid mode (not yet averaging), the "Trailing" status on the panel is active and the Trailing SL automatically locks in basket profit at the Pyramid SL distance from the running price.
Auto Re-EntryA single RE-ENTRY button on the Order Panel — when ON, the EA automatically reopens a position in the same direction after the basket closes, whether through the pyramid trailing stop or the averaging Basket TP.
Target Profit & Max Loss (USD)Automatically closes all positions once the session's total profit (closed + floating) reaches the Target Profit, or when floating loss reaches the Max Loss limit you set (default OFF/disabled).
Spread & AutoTrading Status FilterThe EA only opens new orders while the spread is below the Max Spread limit and the AutoTrading/Algo Trading button in MT5 is enabled — this status is shown in real time on the "Session" row of the panel.
3-Panel Monitoring DisplayEA Setup, Trading Info, and Order Panel show the status, direction, mode (GRID+PYRAMID/GRID ONLY), number of orders, BEP, Basket TP, Floating/Closed/Total P/L, and Trailing status in real time.
Usage Guide

How to Use the EA

  1. Activation with PasswordEnter your Trading Account Number and Activation Password (provided by the admin via email/Telegram after your account is approved as a rebate member) into the EA's input parameters. The EA only runs if the combination matches your account data registered in the Indorebate network.
  2. PreparationAttach the EA to the chart of the pair you want to trade (all pairs supported — Forex, Gold, etc.). Make sure the trading account is in hedging, not netting.
  3. Set Parameters According to Capital & RiskAdjust Base Lot, Multiplier, Grid Distance (averaging distance), Pyramid Distance (pyramid distance), and above all Max Order — never set it larger than your capital can handle.
  4. Set Trailing & Basket TPSet the Pyramid SL (trailing stop distance) for pyramid mode, and the Basket TP for averaging mode, according to your targets and trading style.
  5. Enable ProtectionSet Target Profit (USD) and Max Loss (USD) as automatic safeguards, plus Max Spread so the EA only opens new orders when market conditions are suitable. Make sure the AutoTrading/Algo Trading button in MT5 stays on — its status can be monitored on the "Session" row of the panel.
  6. Manual EntryAnalyze the market according to your strategy, then press the BUY NOW / SELL NOW button on the Order Panel in the direction you believe in. From that point the EA automatically decides between pyramiding or averaging as price moves.
  7. Set Re-Entry as NeededTurn the RE-ENTRY button on/off — leave it ON if you want the EA to automatically reopen a position in the same direction after the basket closes (whether via the pyramid trailing stop or the averaging Basket TP).
  8. Monitor RegularlyCheck the EA panel regularly, especially when the market is volatile (news releases, trading session overlaps). The EA is not a substitute for your own supervision.
  9. Reset Any TimeUse the Close All / Reset button to close all positions and reset the EA state if needed.
