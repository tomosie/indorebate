Features

What This Dashboard Shows

Inspired by the "Technical Summary" widget on Investing.com — summarizing dozens of indicators into one easy-to-read table, right on the MT5 chart.

Column / FeatureDescription
Multi-Timeframe (TF)Shows summaries for several timeframes at once in a single panel — default M5, M15, M30, H1, H4 (M1 is available and can optionally be enabled).
Moving AverageCompares the current price against 6 MA periods (5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200) x 2 methods (SMA & EMA) = 12 signals per timeframe, then summarizes them into one verdict with the (buy/sell) count beside it.
Indicators (Oscillators)Combines 11 oscillator/momentum indicators: RSI, Stochastic, Stochastic RSI, MACD, ADX, Williams %R, CCI, Highs/Lows 14-period, Ultimate Oscillator, Rate of Change, and Bull/Bear Power — each classified as buy/sell/neutral then summarized.
VolatilityCompares the current ATR(14) against the average ATR of the last 20 candles to label it Low, Normal, or High.
Signal (Verdict)The final conclusion per timeframe: combines all Moving Average + Oscillator signals, then gives a verdict of STRONG BUY if ≥65% of signals agree, BUY/SELL if the majority agree, STRONG SELL if ≥65% point the opposite way, or NEUTRAL if balanced.
Auto-UpdateThe panel refreshes automatically every few seconds (default 5 seconds) following the latest market tick, with no manual refresh needed.
Customizable PanelPanel position (4 chart corners), offset distance, font size & type, and buy/sell/neutral colors can be set through the indicator inputs to your taste.
Usage Guide

How to Use & Read the Dashboard

  1. Attach to the ChartDrag the indicator onto any pair's chart (Forex, Gold, indices, crypto — all supported because it is based on standard technical calculations).
  2. Choose the Timeframes to DisplayEnable/disable the M1, M5, M15, M30, H1, H4 rows according to your trading style — scalpers can focus on small TFs, swing traders on large TFs.
  3. Adjust the Panel AppearanceAdjust the position (chart corner), offset, font size, and colors so it does not cover important areas of the chart.
  4. Read the Moving Average & Indicators ColumnsLook at the label (STRONG BUY/BUY/NEUTRAL/SELL/STRONG SELL) and the numbers (buy/sell) beside it — the closer to STRONG, the more indicators agree.
  5. Check Volatility Before EntryThe Volatil. column helps assess whether the market is calm (Low), normal (Normal), or turbulent (High) — useful for adjusting lot size and SL/TP distance.
  6. View the Combined Signal per TimeframeThe Signal column is the final conclusion for each TF. Ideally look for cross-timeframe confirmation — for example H1 & H4 both STRONG SELL — before treating it as an input for an entry decision.
  7. Still Combine It with Your Own AnalysisUse this dashboard as a tool for quickly reading market conditions, not as an automatic entry signal — still pay attention to price action, support/resistance, and economic news.
